Recommendations

  • 🔴 Urgent: Book your staff into a short free cybersecurity awareness session. ZICTA offers resources at zicta.zm. At minimum, train staff to spot fake emails (phishing) and use strong passwords — once per year.
  • 🟡 Improve: Write down who is allowed to access which files or systems. Make sure only the people who need access have it. When a staff member leaves, remove their access the same day. Review the list every 6 months.
  • 🟡 Improve: Write a simple one-page plan: what to do if you are hacked or data is stolen. Include who to call (IT provider, ZICTA on +260 211 369 000, CERT-ZM), what to switch off, and how to inform customers. Practise the plan once a year.
  • 🟡 Improve: Set a PIN or fingerprint lock on all business phones and tablets. Set them to lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Enable 'Find My Device' so you can wipe a lost phone remotely. Only install apps from the official app store.
  • 🔴 Urgent: Keep your router, server, and main computers in a locked room or locked cabinet. Only authorised staff should have the key. Keep a simple visitor log (name, time in, time out) and consider a basic security camera for the IT area.
