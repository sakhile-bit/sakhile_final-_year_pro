# Researcher's Scoring & Analysis Guide
*(remove this section from the copy given to participants)*

### System Usability Scale (U1–U10)
Each item scores 0–4.
- **Odd items (U1, U3, U5, U7, U9):** score = (response − 1)
- **Even items (U2, U4, U6, U8, U10):** score = (5 − response)
- **SUS score = (sum of the 10 item scores) × 2.5** → range 0–100.

Interpretation (Bangor et al. / Sauro): **> 80.3 = A (excellent)**, **68 = average**,
**51–68 = OK/marginal**, **< 51 = poor**. Report mean, SD, and the % of participants above 68.

### Usefulness (V1–V8) and task usability (U11–U15)
Treat as 5-point Likert. Report **mean and SD per item**, plus a combined
"perceived usefulness" mean (V1–V8). Optionally compute % agreement (responses of 4–5).

### Willingness to pay — Van Westendorp (Q14)
Plot cumulative distributions of the four price points across all participants:
- **PMC (Point of Marginal Cheapness):** intersection of "too cheap" and "expensive".
- **PME (Point of Marginal Expensiveness):** intersection of "bargain" and "too expensive".
- **OPP (Optimal Price Point):** intersection of "too cheap" and "too expensive".
- **Range of Acceptable Prices:** between PMC and PME.
Report OPP and the acceptable range in ZMW. Cross-tabulate Q12/Q13/Q15 by business size (Q2).

### Net Promoter / adoption
- **NPS (Q18):** % Promoters (9–10) − % Detractors (0–6).
- **Adoption intent (Q19):** report distribution; treat "Likely/Very likely" as intent-to-adopt.

### Suggested sample & segmentation
Aim for a mix across Part 1 (role, business size, IT confidence). Segment usability and
WTP results by role and business size to surface differences (e.g. owners vs IT staff).

### Ethics reminder
Keep signed consent forms separate from response data; store data securely; report only
aggregates. Confirm alignment with your institution's ethics requirements before collecting.
